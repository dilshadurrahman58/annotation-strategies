Strategies Examples Citation

Five annotation strategies recurring across 72 professional news charts.

This site provides a strategy-indexed companion to Exploring Annotation Strategies in Professional Visualizations: Insights from Prominent US News Portals. It presents each of the five strategies (S1–S5) identified in the paper alongside the figures used to illustrate them, with per-figure annotation analysis.

Five Annotation Strategies

Five recurring strategies identified through qualitative analysis of static charts from The New York Times, The Washington Post, The Wall Street Journal, and The Economist. Select a card to read the full strategy discussion and a representative example.
